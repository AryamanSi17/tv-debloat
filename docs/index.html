Debloat Android TV & Google TV. Remove ads without root.

Make a slow, ad-filled smart TV fast and clean again. tv-debloat switches off manufacturer bloatware, ad services and telemetry over Wi-Fi. Nothing is installed on the TV, and every change can be undone.

Why tv-debloat

Nothing installed on the TV

Everything runs from your computer over ADB. The TV only has apps switched off.

No root needed

Uses pm disable-user on stock, unrooted TVs. Apps are disabled, never uninstalled.

Removes bloat and ads

Manufacturer telemetry, promo pop-ups, content tabs and silent auto-installers.

Safe by default

The TV's home screen app is always skipped, so the Home button keeps working.

Backup & one-command restore

Snapshot the TV before any change and roll back to it any time.

Laptop remote

Drive the TV from your keyboard and type passwords from your laptop.

How to debloat your Android TV

  1. Turn on USB debugging

    On the TV: Settings → System → About → press OK on Build 7 times → back → Developer options → turn on USB debugging. Keep it on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

  2. Clone and open Claude Code

    Run these on your computer:

git clone https://github.com/AryamanSi17/tv-debloat.git
cd tv-debloat
claude

  1. Paste this prompt

    Claude finds your TV, backs it up, asks which apps you use, debloats it and checks every step with screenshots. Accept "Allow USB debugging?" on the TV when it appears.

My Android TV is slow and full of ads. Use the tools in this repo to find it on my Wi-Fi, connect
over ADB, and snapshot it with tools/backup.sh before changing anything. Then debloat it: disable
the manufacturer's ads, telemetry and junk with tools/disable.sh (reversibly, never uninstall),
asking me first about apps and features I might use, and save the list under examples/<my TV model>/.
Don't install anything on the TV and never disable the home screen app. Check every step with
screenshots from the TV, test that Home and my apps still work, and tell me how to roll back.

Note: tools/tv.py talks ADB without the adb binary (macOS blocks it).

Prefer doing it by hand? The manual steps are four commands.

Frequently asked questions

Does debloating Android TV need root?

No. tv-debloat uses ADB over Wi-Fi and pm disable-user, which works on stock, unrooted TVs.

Does it install anything on my TV?

No. All commands run from your computer. Nothing is added to the TV; apps are only switched off.

Is it safe? Can I undo it?

Yes. Apps are disabled, never uninstalled, the home screen app is never touched, and one command restores the backup. A factory reset also re-enables all apps.

How do I remove ads from the Google TV home screen?

Disabling the manufacturer's promo apps and Google's auto-installer removes most of them. For Google TV's own recommendation rows, turn on Apps only mode (Settings → Accounts & Sign In → your account); it also turns off some Google features.

Why is my Android TV slow?

Low-memory TVs, often with 1.5 to 2 GB of RAM, run many manufacturer services in the background. Disabling them frees memory for the apps you actually use.

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV devices including TCL, Xiaomi, Redmi, Mi TV, Hisense, Sony Bravia, OnePlus, Realme, Nvidia Shield and Chromecast with Google TV. Not Samsung, LG or Amazon Fire TV.
